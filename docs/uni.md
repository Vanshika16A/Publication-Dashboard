# Academic units of the assigned universities

Compiled 2026-10-12 from the universities' official websites only (jnu.ac.in, uohyd.ac.in,
du.ac.in and their subdomains). Purpose: decide which units do research with a technical aspect,
in the sense of IIIT Hyderabad's HSRC (computational social science) and CCNSB (computational
natural sciences and bioinformatics).

~~Struck-through~~ units are confirmed non-technical: since 2026-10-12 a paper is dropped when
all its authors are from such units of the assigned universities (D-021; exceptions in brackets
are never used to drop). Department-level names count too (e.g. "Department of History,
Hansraj College").

**Classes**

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tech | Engineering, computing, computational or data science, nanotechnology, or research done mainly with technical or computational methods |
| Mixed | A substantial technical strand inside a non-technical or experimental unit |
| Sci | Natural sciences, mainly experimental (technical or not is an open decision) |
| Med | Medicine and health |
| Non | Humanities, social sciences, languages, arts, law, commerce, management, education |

**Papers** is the number of papers in build 20261008T211843Z whose affiliation text names the
unit (spelling variants merged). Many papers name no unit, so these are lower bounds; "–" means
not counted.

(https://fmsc.du.ac.in/), the colleges list (https://www.du.ac.in/index.php?page=colleges-at-du,

about an individual paper; a department within a college ("Department of Physics, Hansraj
