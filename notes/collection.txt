we only downloaded from within science and engineering fields through the openalex api.

this includes the following openalex primary topics:
- agricultural and biological sciences
- biochemistry, genetics and molecular biology
- chemical engineering
- chemistry
- computer science
- earth and planetary sciences
- energy
- engineering
- environmental science
- immunology and microbiology
- materials science
- mathematics
- neuroscience
- physics and astronomy

we did not include:
- medicine, nursing, dentistry, health professions, veterinary
- pharmacology, toxicology and pharmaceutics
- arts and humanities, social sciences, psychology
- business, management and accounting
- economics, econometrics and finance
- decision sciences

each paper has one primary topic in openalex. we used that one to decide if a paper is in or out.

we downloaded the following types of papers:
- article
- conference paper

we included all of the above published between 2016 and 2026.

we only downloaded papers with at least one author from:
- jawaharlal nehru university (openalex id I152429107)
- university of hyderabad (openalex id I36893310)
- university of delhi (openalex id I110166357)

this also counts their colleges and centres.

the download was done on 13 october 2026. citation counts are as of that day.

this gave us 38,761 papers:
- 35,233 articles
- 3,528 conference papers

papers per institution:
- university of delhi: 23,830
- jawaharlal nehru university: 8,346
- university of hyderabad: 7,683

(these add up to more than 38,761 because 1,097 papers have authors from two or more of
the three institutions.)

papers per year:
- 2016: 2,878
- 2017: 2,920
- 2018: 3,091
- 2019: 3,007
- 2020: 3,327
- 2021: 3,724
- 2022: 3,839
- 2023: 4,060
- 2024: 4,278
- 2025: 4,326
- 2026: 3,311 (partial obviously)

1,118 of these papers have 100 or more authors. openalex only gives the first 100 authors in
a normal search, so we downloaded the full author list for these papers one by one.

along with the papers, we also downloaded:
- author profiles (h-index, orcid) for 20,627 authors
- 172,436 indian papers that our papers cite
- 138,740 indian papers that cite our papers
- details for 6,938 journals and conferences
- the core conference ranking list (for conference ranks)

these numbers are from the download itself, before any cleaning.
