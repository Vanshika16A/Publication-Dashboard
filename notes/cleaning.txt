we started from the 38,761 papers in the download (see collection.txt).

cleaning happens in two steps:
- build: turns the papers into csv rows and removes duplicates and wrong affiliations
- clean: removes papers that are out of scope or are not research, and tidies names and titles

every removed paper or row is saved in a review file, so it can be checked by hand.
build review files: collector/data/output/build/review/
clean review files: collector/data/output/review/


=== build ===

1. only real journal and conference papers

openalex calls some things "article" that are not in a journal or conference.
we removed 512 papers:
- 361 only in a repository
- 145 with no journal or conference at all
- 6 in a book series

a paper is a "journal" paper if it is an article in a journal.
a paper is a "conference" paper if it is a conference paper, or an article in a conference.

left: 38,249 papers


2. duplicate papers

sometimes openalex has the same paper twice.
- same doi: 1 paper removed
- same title, same year and at least one same author: 126 papers removed

when two copies were found, we kept the one with the most citations.

left: 38,122 papers


3. one row per author

the csv has one row for each (paper, institution, author).
so a paper by 3 du authors gives 3 rows.
this gave 97,901 rows.


4. checking affiliations

openalex sometimes puts an author at one of our institutions when the author did not write it.
we only kept a row if the author's own affiliation text on that paper names the institution.
we removed 44 rows:
- 27 named a look-alike institution
  (e.g. "OU, Hyderabad" is osmania university, not university of hyderabad.
  jncasr and jntu are not jnu.)
- 17 had no affiliation text at all on that paper

17 papers lost all their authors this way, so they were removed too.

left: 38,105 papers


5. same person more than once

some people have two or more openalex ids. if two ids have the same orcid, we treated them as
one person. this merged 272 ids.
after that:
- 718 rows were the same person twice on the same paper: removed
- 131 rows were exactly the same as another row in every column: removed

left after build: 38,105 papers, 97,008 rows


=== clean ===

6. journals outside science and engineering

openalex sometimes tags a medical paper as biology, so it passed the download filter.
scimago lists the subject areas of every journal. if none of a journal's areas is one of our
14 fields, we removed its papers.
we removed 1,874 papers, mostly from medical journals (pediatrics, dermatology, oncology,
neurology, etc.), pharmacy, social sciences, business, law and humanities journals.


7. venues that are not research

we removed 171 papers from:
- chemInform (it only re-lists papers published elsewhere)
- books of meeting abstracts (spast abstracts, goldschmidt abstracts and others)
- library and law venues that scimago does not list (library herald, library hi tech news,
  yearbook of international environmental law)


8. papers that are not research

- 34 retracted or withdrawn papers
- 13 notices: errata, prefaces, editorials, "50 years ago in" columns


9. authors that are not people

3 rows had a group as the author (e.g. "on behalf of the cms collaboration"): removed.


10. tidying names and titles (nothing removed)

author names (1,141 rows changed):
- removed footnote numbers and orcid text ("sonker1" -> "sonker")
- removed dr., prof., mr., ms.
- fixed names written twice ("shilpi gupta shilpi gupta" changed to "shilpi gupta")
- ALL CAPS names in normal case ("VED PRAKASH GUPTA" -> "Ved Prakash Gupta")
  (initials like "MS" or "D. K." are kept as they are.)

titles (213 rows changed):
- removed one full stop at the end ("... in the rat." -> "... in the rat")
  (not after "et al." or "sp.")


=== final data ===

36,013 papers, 92,219 rows:
- 32,517 journal papers
- 3,496 conference papers

papers per institution:
- university of delhi: 21,856 (58,480 rows)
- jawaharlal nehru university: 7,895 (16,621 rows)
- university of hyderabad: 7,324 (17,118 rows)

quartiles:
- 29,526 of the 32,517 journal papers have a scimago quartile (91%)
  (3,522 of these use the nearest scimago year, e.g. 2026 papers use 2025.)
- 1,179 of the 3,496 conference papers have a rank (98 from core, the rest are scimago
  quartiles of the proceedings series they are published in)


=== known gaps (not fixed) ===

- 6,115 rows have no openalex author id, so they have no h-index.
- 5,253 rows have cites_indian_inst blank, because openalex has no reference list for the paper.
- 1,061 papers have 100 or more authors. they are kept, and marked in the extended csv.
- openalex cannot tell faculty from students, so authors are "affiliated authors".
- two different people with the same openalex id cannot be found.
